## Business Rules Covered

- The account starts at `1000.00` for each new application process.
- A credit adds the entered amount and persists the result in memory.
- A debit succeeds when the current balance is greater than or equal to the entered amount.
- A debit greater than the current balance is rejected without changing the balance.
- Successful changes are visible to later operations in the same process.
- The balance is not persisted after the application exits.
- Menu choices outside `1` through `4` are rejected; option `4` exits.
- Negative values, zero values, malformed input, and numeric overflow are not explicitly validated by the current COBOL source and therefore require stakeholder decisions during migration.
